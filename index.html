Big shade trees at the foot of the Tushars

A family-run campground in Beaver, Utah, with sites for campers, trailers, motor homes and tents. Dinner from Maria's kitchen is a short walk from your site.

Call to reserve a site Get directions

Look for the red sign on 200 North

Beaver Canyon Campground sits where the road starts climbing toward the Tushar Mountains, just east of downtown Beaver. It's an easy stop off I-15 and a comfortable base for a longer stay in the canyon.

Pull in under the old cottonwoods, set up on level gravel, and you're close to fishing, trails, and Eagle Point. You can also stay put and enjoy the shade and the mountain air.

Up the canyon and around Beaver

The canyon road climbs from just east of camp into the Fishlake National Forest. Here's what's close by.

About 18 mi east

Eagle Point Resort

Skiing in winter, plus lift-served mountain biking and summer events high in the Tushars.

Right out the gate

Beaver River

Trout fishing and shady pullouts all the way up the canyon road.

About 20 mi east

Puffer Lake

A high alpine lake for fishing, paddling and cool summer afternoons.

Up the canyon

Fishlake National Forest

Hiking, OHV trails and wildflower meadows across the Tushar range.

About 15 mi west

Minersville Reservoir

Boating and year-round fishing in a big open-sky setting.

Minutes away

Historic Beaver

Old stone buildings, the courthouse museum, and famous local cheese curds.
